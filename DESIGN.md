# MoaPick design direction

References reviewed 2026-11-13:
- https://linear.app — persistent navigation, restrained controls, clear lists.
- https://www.notion.com/product/calendar — calendar-first information hierarchy and compact date navigation.
- https://identityforge.io/learn/make-ai-ui-less-generic — generic gradients, uniform card grids and inconsistent hierarchy are common pitfalls, not universal prohibitions.

Use a quiet workspace: warm off-white canvas, pale sidebar, forest primary controls, limited terracotta accent. Date numerals, typography and whitespace establish hierarchy. No decorative gradients, glass panels, oversized login slogans after sign-in or repetitive numbered onboarding. Adapt layout principles rather than copying brand assets.

Menus: events, subscriptions, upload, history, settings. Review is a contextual detail page. Mobile navigation remains visible. Controls have keyboard focus, textual labels and explicit deletion confirmation. Preserve important consent, errors and deletion consequences; hide infrastructure details from everyday flows.
